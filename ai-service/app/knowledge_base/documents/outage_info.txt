Title: Network Outage Information
Category: NetworkOutage

How to identify and respond to network outages.

Signs of a network outage vs a local issue:
- If ONLY your connection is down and neighbors have internet, it's likely a local issue (router, cable, or account problem).
- If multiple people in your area report no internet, it's likely a network outage.
- If the router's "Internet/WAN" light is red or off, but "Power" and "LAN" lights are normal, it's likely an upstream issue.

What to do during a confirmed outage:
1. Check the NetDesk portal or app for outage notifications in your area.
2. Do NOT repeatedly restart your router — this won't help during an outage and may cause configuration issues.
3. If you need urgent internet access, consider using mobile hotspot as a temporary solution.
4. Outages are typically resolved within 2-6 hours. Major infrastructure outages may take up to 24 hours.

What our team does during an outage:
- Network Operations Center (NOC) is automatically alerted when multiple customers in an area go offline.
- Field teams are dispatched if the issue is physical infrastructure (cable cuts, equipment failure).
- Software/configuration issues are resolved remotely by the NOC team.

After an outage is resolved:
- Your connection should restore automatically. If it doesn't within 30 minutes of the outage being marked resolved, restart your router once.
- If service doesn't return after restart, contact support — your connection may need manual re-provisioning.
