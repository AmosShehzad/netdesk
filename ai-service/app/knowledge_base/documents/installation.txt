Title: New Connection Installation Guide
Category: Installation

Everything about getting a new internet connection installed.

Installation timeline:
- Urban areas: 2-3 business days after order confirmation.
- Suburban areas: 3-5 business days.
- Rural areas: 5-10 business days (may require additional infrastructure).

What to expect on installation day:
1. A technician will visit your premises at the scheduled time (a 2-hour window will be communicated).
2. Someone over 18 must be present at the address to authorize the installation.
3. The technician will install the fiber/cable entry point, set up the router, and test the connection.
4. Installation typically takes 1-2 hours.

Requirements for installation:
- Valid government ID of the account holder.
- Access to the building's cable entry point (for apartments, coordinate with building management).
- A power outlet near the desired router location.
- The first month's bill and installation fee must be paid before the visit.

After installation:
- Default WiFi name and password are on the sticker under your router.
- Change the default password through the router admin panel (192.168.1.1) for security.
- Download the NetDesk app to manage your account, pay bills, and contact support.

Rescheduling or cancelling:
- You can reschedule up to 24 hours before the appointment through the portal or by calling support.
- If you're not present during the scheduled visit, a rescheduling fee may apply.
